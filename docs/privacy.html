Privacy Policy

Steady Noise, an Alexa skill. Effective November 8, 2026.

What the skill collects

Nothing that identifies you. Steady Noise keeps no account, no database, and no history of what you play. The few details it needs to continue playback (which color, where in the track, and how much of a timer is left) travel inside the playback token that Alexa passes back to the skill, and are not stored by the skill.

The skill does not request any Alexa permissions and never receives your name, email address, location, contacts, or voice recordings. Your interactions with Alexa are handled by Amazon under Amazon's privacy notice; you can review and delete them in the Alexa app.

Purchases

Steady Noise Premium is sold and billed by Amazon. The skill learns only whether your account is subscribed; it never sees payment details. Manage or cancel the subscription in the Alexa app or by saying "Alexa, ask Steady Noise to cancel my subscription."

Audio delivery

Your Echo device downloads the audio files directly from the web host that serves them. Like any web server, that host may briefly log standard request data (such as IP address and time) for operations and abuse prevention. Those logs are not used to identify or profile listeners.

Operational logs

Errors are logged by the skill's hosting (Amazon Web Services) for troubleshooting. These logs contain request types and error messages, not personal information.

Children

The skill is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

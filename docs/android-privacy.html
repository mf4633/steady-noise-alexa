Privacy Policy

Steady Noise: White Noise, the Android app on Google Play. Effective November 11, 2026.

AppSteady Noise: White Noise
Packagecom.hydroengineer.steadynoise
DeveloperStone Lake Application Development (Google Play developer name)

This page covers the Android app only. The Alexa skill has its own privacy policy.

What the app collects

Nothing. Every sound is generated live on your phone, so nothing is downloaded or streamed while you listen. The app keeps only your last sleep-timer and volume settings, in its private storage on the phone.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files. It asks for permission to show notifications so the playback controls can appear on your lock screen; you can say no and playback still works.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes its settings.

Purchases

Steady Noise Premium sounds is a one-time in-app purchase sold and billed by Google Play. The app asks Google Play only whether you own it; it never sees your name, email address, or payment details. Google handles the purchase under the Google Privacy Policy.

No ads, no analytics, no accounts

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. There is no sign-in and no account. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, and nothing is used to build a profile.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
